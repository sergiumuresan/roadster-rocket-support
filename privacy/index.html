ROADSTER ROCKET

Roadster Rocket privacy policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Roadster Rocket is provided by Perpetuum Mobile.

Information used by the game

Roadster Rocket saves your tutorial progress, best scores and distances, earned coins, run counters used for achievements, the last leaderboard list shown, selected environment, and audio, haptic and graphics settings in the app's local storage. The game uses these records to restore your progress and preferences. You do not need an account to play.

This version of the game does not send your gameplay records or personal information to us. It has no advertising, online multiplayer, in-app purchases or developer-operated server, and no analytics other than the event counts described under Google Play Games below. The Android app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising-ID permissions. Two optional features, described below, pass information to other services only when you use them: sharing a result, and Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements.

The optional vibration permission supports haptic feedback. You can turn haptics off in Settings. It does not give the game access to personal data.

Android and the game engine can produce local diagnostic logs when an error occurs. The game does not upload those logs. If you choose to contact us with diagnostic information, we use what you send to respond to your request.

Sharing a result

The results screen and the title screen have Share buttons. When you tap one, the game makes a picture (of that result with the view of your run, your score and distance, or of your leaderboard rank, or of your achievements) and hands it, with a short message and a link to the game's store page, to Android's share sheet. Nothing is sent until you pick an app there, and the game does not learn which app you chose or whether you sent anything. The app you pick handles the picture and message under its own privacy policy.

The picture is stored in the app's local storage and replaced the next time you share. The store link carries a fixed label saying the visit came from a shared result. It is the same for every player and does not identify you.

Google Play Games leaderboards and achievements

Leaderboards and achievements are optional and use Google Play Games Services. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your Endless scores to Google, which can include your best score already saved on your device, and can show Google's leaderboard screen. It receives this week's top scores with those players' gamer names from Google, shows them on the title screen and keeps the last list in local storage. It also sends the achievements you have earned and your progress toward them, and counts of a few in-game events: finished runs on each route, First Flight completions, revives used and how often the share sheet was opened. After each Endless run it also sends that run's figures (score, distance, time, top speed, close calls, stunts, coins and route), from which Google keeps the lifetime statistics shown on your Play Games gamer profile. Google reports these to us as overall statistics in Google Play Console. Other players can then see your Play Games gamer name, avatar, score, rank and achievements, according to the visibility settings of your Play Games profile.

Google operates this service and processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not run a server and do not receive your Google account details. If you are not signed in, the game asks you to sign in only when you open a leaderboard or the achievements, and you can decline and keep playing; your scores and progress then stay on your device.

You can change who sees your activity, or delete your Play Games data for this game, in the Google Play Games app or in your Google Account settings.

Local storage and deletion

Your game records remain in local app storage until you clear that storage or remove the app. Clearing Roadster Rocket's app storage in Android Settings erases your local progress, preferences and the last shared picture. There is no developer-hosted account or cloud save to delete. Scores, achievements and event counts already sent to Google Play Games are removed through Google, as described above.

The Android application manifest disables automatic app backup. Device transfer, operating-system diagnostics, store services and backup tools supplied separately by your device manufacturer or by you are governed by their own settings and policies.

Links you choose to open

Font attribution and license buttons can open websites in your browser. Those websites and your browser handle any resulting network traffic under their own privacy policies. The game does not embed a tracking web view or send your game records with these links.
