ROADSTER ROCKET

Roadster Rocket privacy policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Roadster Rocket is provided by Perpetuum Mobile.

Information used by the game

Roadster Rocket saves your tutorial progress, best scores and distances, earned coins, selected environment and difficulty, and audio, haptic and graphics settings in the app's local storage. The game uses these records to restore your progress and preferences. You do not need an account to play.

This version of the game does not send your gameplay records or personal information to us. It has no advertising, analytics, account service, online multiplayer, in-app purchases or developer-operated server. The Android app does not request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or advertising-ID permissions.

The optional vibration permission supports haptic feedback. You can turn haptics off in Settings. It does not give the game access to personal data.

Android and the game engine can produce local diagnostic logs when an error occurs. The game does not upload those logs. If you choose to contact us with diagnostic information, we use what you send to respond to your request.

Local storage and deletion

Your game records remain in local app storage until you clear that storage or remove the app. Clearing Roadster Rocket's app storage in Android Settings erases your local progress and preferences. There is no developer-hosted account or cloud save to delete.

The Android application manifest disables automatic app backup. Device transfer, operating-system diagnostics, store services and backup tools supplied separately by your device manufacturer or by you are governed by their own settings and policies.

Links you choose to open

Font attribution and license buttons can open websites in your browser. Those websites and your browser handle any resulting network traffic under their own privacy policies. The game does not embed a tracking web view or send your game records with these links.

Contact

If you email us, your email provider and ours process that correspondence. We receive the email address and information you choose to include so that we can respond. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if the game's data practices change. Any future online service, advertising or analytics integration requires a new review of this policy and the store's Data safety declaration before release.
